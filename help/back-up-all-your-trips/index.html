Help iCloud, privacy & Pro

Back up all your trips

Download one file with everything you keep in Trovelo. Restore it later, on this iPhone or on a new one.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad
1

Open Backup & Restore

Tap the round profile button at the top right. Under Your Data, tap Backup & Restore. It shows when you last made one.

1Backup & Restore
2

Download a Backup

Tap Download a Backup and choose where to keep the file, like Files or iCloud Drive. It holds your trips, documents, spots and settings.

1Download
3

Restore from a Backup

Tap Restore from a Backup…, pick the file and choose what comes back. It adds what’s missing and never deletes what you already have.

1Restore

Good to know

Trovelo Pro isn’t in the file: it goes with your Apple Account.

Shared trips come back unticked, because restoring one would change the plan for everyone in it.

Backups are free, with or without Pro.
