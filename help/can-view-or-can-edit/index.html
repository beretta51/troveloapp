Help Plan together

Can view or can edit?

Each person you invite can view the trip or edit it. You can change that, or take them out, at any time.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad
1

What each one means

Can view: they see the whole trip, but can’t change it. Can edit: they add, move and delete cards, like you.

1The people in the trip
2

Change it any time

Open Together and tap Can view or Can edit next to a person’s name to switch it. Remove takes them out of the trip.

3

Leave or stop sharing

Stop sharing ends it for everyone: they keep the copy they have, but stop getting your changes. A guest can Leave this trip, and the cards they added stay for everyone else.

Good to know

In a trip you can only view, the group’s expenses show without their buttons.
