Change the order of your day

A day runs top to bottom. Put the cards in the order you’ll do them, and the map numbers them the same way.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad Mac
1

Open Optimize Day

Touch and hold any card of the day and choose Optimize Day…. The Optimize button over the map opens the same screen.

1Optimize Day…2Or here
2

Drag them into place

Hold the lines on the right of a card and drag it up or down. The numbers on the map follow. Tap ✓ to keep the new order, or ✕ to leave the day as it was.

1Drag here2Save
1

Drag it up or down

On the Mac, drag a card up or down in its column. Drop it in another column to change its day too.
