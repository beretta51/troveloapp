Drag cards between days

On the Mac, the Board is made for dragging: every column is a day.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 Mac
1

Drag a card to another day

Drag any card to another column to change its day, or up and down to change its place. The Unplanned column holds the ideas without a day.

1
1Drag it
2

Add from the bottom of a day

Under each column, + Add card adds a place, and the two buttons next to it log an expense or write a note.

1
1Add card, expense, note
3

From the keyboard

The same things, without the mouse, and the Board the size you like:

⌘NNew card ⇧⌘ELog expense ⌃⌘NNew note ⌥⌘1Normal cards ⌥⌘2Compact cards ⇧⌘WWeather
