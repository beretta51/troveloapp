Every kind of card

A card can be a sight, a meal, a hotel, a flight or a ticket. Its kind sets its icon, its color and the details it asks for.

2 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad
1

Open the card’s category

Open a card and tap the pencil. At the top are the five kinds you use most. Tap All to see every one.

1Every kind2The five you use most
2

Search or browse

Kinds are grouped: stays, places and sights, food and drink, activities, entertainment, travel, money and other. Or just type what it is.

1Search2Grouped by kind
3

Some kinds ask for more

A stay asks for check-in and check-out, its confirmation number and the door code. A flight asks for its number and airports, and a train or a ferry for its stations, coach and seat.

1Check-in and out2Confirmation3Door code

Good to know

Flights have their own form, with every stop. See Add a flight with layovers.

Notes are written from the note button at the bottom of the Board, not from this list.
