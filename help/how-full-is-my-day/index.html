How full is my day?

Every day adds up the time at each place and the time to get between them, and shows how much of your day it fills.

2 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad Mac
1

Read the bar

Under the day’s name, each block is one hour of your day. Easy day means at least half is still free, Busy day means less than half is. When it no longer fits, it says Doesn’t fit and the extra hours turn orange.

1One block per hour2Easy or busy3Planned so far4What’s left
2

What counts

The time at each place, in grey above its name, plus the time to get from one card to the next. Notes, expenses and hotel nights take no time.

1Getting there2Time at the place
3

Set your own pace

Tap the time left to open the trip’s settings. Pick Easy (6 hours), Normal (9) or Packed (12), or Off to hide the bar for this trip.

1Hours a day2The default, or no bar
1

The same bar on every column

Each day’s column shows the same blocks, the time planned and the time left under its date.

1
1How full the day is

Good to know

Each kind of card has a usual length. To change one card’s, open it, tap the pencil and type the minutes next to the hourglass.

Short hops are always walked. Longer ones use the transport you set in the trip’s settings.
