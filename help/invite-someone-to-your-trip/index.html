Help Plan together

Invite someone to your trip

Plan the trip with the people coming on it. You add each one by name, and nobody else can get in.

2 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad
1

Open Together

Open the trip’s Overview and tap the people button at the top. Or touch and hold the trip in your list and choose Share Trip….

1Together2Or Share Trip…
2

Tap Share this trip

Until you do, nobody else can open the trip. The bottom of the sheet says what’s never shared.

1Share this trip
3

Add each person

Add each person by the email or phone number they use for iCloud, and choose Can view or Can edit. Send them the link: it opens for them and nobody else.

4

Plan together

Once they open it, the trip is in their Trovelo too. Cards anyone adds, moves or deletes show up for everyone.

Good to know

There is no public link. Each link opens only for the people you added.

Only the person who shared the trip can add or remove people.

Trovelo for Mac shares trips and opens invitations too.
