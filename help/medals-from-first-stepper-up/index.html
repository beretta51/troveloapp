Medals, from First Stepper up

Every country you add to Spots counts toward 13 medals, from First Stepper to Apex Traveler.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad
1

See your medal

In Spots, drag the sheet all the way up. Your medal is at the top, with how many countries you need for the next one. Share my medal sends it to a friend.

1Your rank2Share my medal
2

The 13 ranks

Each one needs a number of countries:

  1. First Stepper1
  2. Explorer3
  3. Wanderer5
  4. Wayfarer10
  5. Voyager15
  6. Pathfinder20
  7. Nomad30
  8. Globetrotter40
  9. World Citizen55
  10. Continent Hunter70
  11. Odyssey Master85
  12. Centurion100
  13. Apex Traveler120
3

Your travel life

Below your medal, a line runs year by year, with the countries of each year and the medals you won in it. Tap a year to see its places.

What’s still ahead

At the end of the line, your next medal shows how many countries are left, and the closest ones you haven’t added yet.
