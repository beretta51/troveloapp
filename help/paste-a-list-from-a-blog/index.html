Help Guides & Someday

Paste a list from a blog

Got a list from a blog, a friend or ChatGPT? Paste it, and each place lands on your day with its spot on the map.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad Mac
1

Tap Paste your list

Open the day and scroll to the end. Tap Paste your list.

1Paste your list
2

One place per line

Paste the names, one per line. Check the day at the top, then tap ✓.

1The day2Your list3Add them
3

Find them on your day

Trovelo finds each one near your city and adds it with its spot on the map. One it can’t find is still added, so you can fix it by hand.

1Added2On your day
4

No list yet?

Copy prompt copies a ready-made question about your trip. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, and paste the answer back here.

1Copy prompt
1

Paste a list on the Mac

Copy the list, then choose Edit › Paste a List into Someday. The places wait in Someday, by city, until you plan a trip there.
