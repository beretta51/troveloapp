Plugs and voltage

Your packing list shows the plug and the voltage where you’re going, and tells you if you need an adapter.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad
1

Look at the top of Packing

The card at the top of your packing list shows the plug types and the voltage of the country you’re going to, and whether your plugs fit.

1Plugs and voltage
2

Tap it for the details

Each plug is drawn, so you know it when you see it. Trovelo compares them with the plugs where you live, from your iPhone’s region.

1The plugs there2Do yours fit?
3

When yours don’t fit

It says Adapter needed and shows your plug next to theirs. One tap adds a travel adapter to your list. If the voltage is different, it tells you that too.

Good to know

No internet needed: the plugs of about 190 countries ship with Trovelo.
