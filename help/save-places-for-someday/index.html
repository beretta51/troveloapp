Help Guides & Someday

Save places for someday

A restaurant a friend told you about, a beach you saw online: keep it in Someday. When you plan a trip there, it’s waiting for you.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad Mac
1

Open Someday

Tap the bookmark in the bar at the bottom, then Where would you love to go?

1Search2Someday
2

Save it

Search for the place and tap + next to it. It’s saved under its city. Can’t find it? Save the words as they are, or drop a pin on the map.

1Search2Save it3Saved4Under its city
3

No idea where to go?

Open the search without typing. Amazing places shows six spots from around the world. Tap Shuffle for six more.

1Shuffle
4

Sorted by country

Your places are sorted by country, then city. Tap a country at the top to jump to it.

1Countries2One country
5

Add them to a trip

Plan a trip to a city with saved places and its Unplanned column tells you. Tap Add all, or add just one. They stay in Someday too, for next time.

1

Someday on the Mac

Choose Someday in the sidebar. Copy a whole list and choose Edit › Paste a List into Someday to save every place at once.

⇧⌘VPaste a list into Someday

Good to know

Added them to a trip by mistake? Tap Undo right after.

Someday syncs to your other devices through iCloud.
