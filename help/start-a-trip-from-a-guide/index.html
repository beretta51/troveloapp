Help Guides & Someday

Start a trip from a guide

Guides are ready-made trips, day by day. Pick one, choose the day you start, and it becomes your own trip to change as you like.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad Mac
1

Open Guides

Tap the compass in the bar at the bottom. Filter by how many days you have, or by region.

1Filters2Guides
2

Look inside first

Tap a guide to see the route on the map and every stop, with its photo and its note. Tap a day in the strip to jump to it.

1The days2Every stop
3

Choose the day you start

Pick your start date and tap Create trip. Every day, place and note is copied into a trip of your own.

1Start date2Create trip
4

No dates yet?

Tap Plan without dates to start it with Day 1, Day 2 and so on.

1

Guides on the Mac

Choose Guides in the sidebar. A guide opens with its map on one side and every stop on the other, so you can read it all before you create the trip.

Good to know

It’s your trip now: move, delete or add anything. The guide stays as it was.

Guides marked PRO come with Trovelo Pro.
