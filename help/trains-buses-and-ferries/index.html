Trains, buses and ferries

Trains, buses and ferries get a card that reads like the ticket: stations, times, platform, coach and seat.

2 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad
1

Type where from and where to

Tap + Add and type the route, like Tokyo to Kyoto, or tap Train under the search. For a bus or a ferry, tap More.

1The route2Or tap Train
2

Fill in the ticket

Pick Direct, 1 change or 2 changes, then the train number, the stations and the times. Platform, coach and seat are there too.

1Changes2Train number
3

Price and booking

Add the price in any currency and the booking code. The price goes into your Expenses, not on the card.

1Currency2Booking
4

Your ticket, at a glance

Tap the card on the Board to see the times, coach, seat and platform, and copy the confirmation.

1Coach, seat, platform2Confirmation

Good to know

The map draws the line from one station to the other, with its travel time.

A rental car has its own card too: the pick-up and the return land on their days.
