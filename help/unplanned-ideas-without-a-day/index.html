Ideas without a day

Not every place needs a day yet. Unplanned holds them inside the trip until you decide.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad Mac
1

Open Unplanned

Tap the tray next to the days. Its number tells you how many ideas are waiting.

1Unplanned2Ideas without a day
2

Add ideas to it

With Unplanned open, tap + Add. What you add stays here until you give it a day.

1+ Add
3

Give it a day

Touch and hold an idea and choose a day, or Pick a Date…. Any card on a day can come back with Move to Unplanned.

1Tap the day
1

Drag it onto a day

On the Mac, Unplanned has its own column. Drag an idea from it onto any day.

Good to know

Ideas for a city you haven’t booked yet? Keep them in Someday instead.
