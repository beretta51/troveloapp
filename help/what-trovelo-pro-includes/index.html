What Trovelo Pro includes

Trovelo is free to use. Pro is a single purchase that lifts the limits and adds a few extras. There’s no subscription.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad Mac
1

What’s free

All of Trovelo, with up to two upcoming trips at a time and 50 cards in each. Past trips don’t count. Documents, backups and the Mac app are free for everyone.

2

What Pro adds

Unlimited trips and unlimited cards, the Pro guides, connecting your AI assistant to Trovelo on the Mac, and every Pro feature that comes later.

3

Get it

Tap the round profile button at the top right, then the Trovelo Pro card at the top of Settings. Pay once, through the App Store.

4

Check your plan

Under About, Plan says Free or Pro. Have a code? On the free plan, tap Plan to redeem it.

1Plan

Good to know

Pro goes with your Apple Account, on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. If it doesn’t show on one, tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen.

It isn’t a subscription: nothing renews and nothing is charged again.
