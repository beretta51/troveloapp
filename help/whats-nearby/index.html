What’s nearby

The map shows what’s around your stops. Tap a kind of place, pick one, and it joins your day.

1 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad
1

Open the map

On the Board, tap the map button at the bottom right. The whole day is on the map, with the time between stops.

1Map
2

Pick a kind of place

Tap Food, Coffee, Sights or Bars at the top. Swipe for a hotel, an ATM, a pharmacy or a market. Tap it again to clear the map.

1Kinds of places2What’s around
3

Add it to your day

Tap a place to see it, then tap +. Looking for something specific? Tap Find a place… at the bottom.

1Add2Search

Good to know

Hide unplanned on the map hides the ideas that have no day yet.

With a rental car in the trip, the map also finds parking and gas.
